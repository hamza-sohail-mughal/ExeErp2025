Is update mein kya naya hai:

1) ITEM SEARCH (F1) mein item ka naam ab zyada jagah par poora nazar aata hai. Baqi columns thore patle kar diye gaye hain taake cashier ko lamba naam kata hua na mile.
   - 'Article No' ka column ab shuru mein band hota hai, kyun ke aksar dukanon mein ye khali rehta tha.
   - Agar aap article number rakhte hain to Shop Information > Sale mein 'Item search mein Article No column dikhayen' par tick laga den. Ye Items Record screen par bhi lagu hota hai.
   - Article number se search pehle ki tarah kaam karti hai, column band ho tab bhi.

2) LICENSE STATUS screen par 'Change License' dabane ke baad app ab atak (hang) nahi hoti. Naya license check hote waqt screen par likha aata hai ke check ho raha hai.

3) License ki muddat baqi ho to app ab adaigi (payment) ke message ki wajah se nahi rukti. License khatam ho jaye to app vendor se raabta karne ka kehti hai.
