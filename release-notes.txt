Is update mein kya naya hai:

1) Ab aap faisla kar sakte hain ke kaun bills aur vouchers ko Post ya Unpost kare.
   - Roles ki Configuration mein Post Any Transactions aur Unpost Transactions ki alag ijazat dein.
   - Sirf ijazat wale user ko mutaliqa Post ya Unpost option nazar aata hai.

2) Posted sale invoice ab Update ki ijazat hone par bhi edit nahi hota.
   - Unposted bill apni Update ki ijazat ke mutabiq badal sakte hain.
   - Posted bill par Action se Unpost Invoice karne ke liye alag ijazat chahiye.

3) Dashboard par Sale Record se posted aur unposted dono bills nazar aate hain.
   - Post Sales se sirf unposted bills khulte hain aur All Users pehle se select hota hai.
   - Kai unposted bills tick karke Post Selected Invoices se aik saath post kar sakte hain; Unpost aik bill ka hi hota hai.

4) Bulk Cash / Bank Receipts mein aik hi party ko kai martaba add kar sakte hain.
   - Har entry ka amount, tafseel aur Receive In alag rakh sakte hain.
   - Receive In ke hisab se raqam ka jama aur kul raqam neeche list mein nazar aati hai.
   - Save se pehle accounts aur raqam ki tasdeeq hoti hai; dropdown ki pareshani bhi door ki gayi hai.

5) Salesman Recovery Report mein aik entry wale group ka fazool subtotal ab nahi aata.
   - Kai entries wale group ka subtotal aur aakhri total nazar aata hai.

6) Cash + Bank report mein accounts badalne par pehle se ki hui selection mehfooz rehti hai.
   - Khaas users select karke unki entries ki report bhi dekh sakte hain.

7) Ghair zaroori bank, supplier, expense aur doosre accounts ko inactive ya dobara active kar sakte hain.
   - Nayi entries ki list mein sirf active accounts aate hain.

8) Cash / Bank receipt aur payment mein account dhoondhne ka search option diya gaya hai.

9) Admin ijazat de to user pichhli ya agli tarikh mein entry kar sakta hai.
   - Baghair ijazat wala user aaj ki tarikh par kaam karta hai.
